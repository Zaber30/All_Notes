### Recommended Learning Order

1. `vector`
2. `string`
3. `pair`
4. `stack`
5. `queue`
6. `priority_queue`
7. `set`
8. `unordered_set`
9. `map`
10. `unordered_map`
11. Iterators
12. STL Algorithms
13. Custom Comparators
14. Lambda Expressions
15. `deque`
16. Numeric Algorithms (`accumulate`, `gcd`, `iota`)
17. `multiset` / `multimap`
18. `bitset`
19. Heap Algorithms

These topics cover **over 90% of STL usage** in coding interviews and competitive programming.
